01 · About

Research interests

My research uses field experiments, administrative data, and economic theory to study social protection, labour markets, and firm behaviour.

I am currently a Predoctoral Research Fellow at the Oxford Centre for Macro-Experimental Development and the Centre for the Study of African Economies. I will begin a PhD in Economics at MIT in 2027.

  1. 01

    Labour markets & gender

    Job search, workplace institutions, regulation, and women’s employment.

  2. 02

    Social protection

    Safety nets, economic inclusion programmes, livelihoods, and social cohesion.

  3. 03

    Climate & development

    Household adaptation to climate shocks and the role of public programmes.

02 · Research

Selected work

Full research record ↗
Published Scottish Journal of Political Economy · 2025
01

Measuring Workplace Sexual Harassment: A Review ↗

With Abi Adams

Examines how survey design changes measured prevalence, showing that explicit behavioural examples and detailed definitions reveal substantially more workplace harassment.

Published Journal of Population Economics · 2024
02

An Indian Enigma? Labour Market Impacts of the World’s Largest Livelihoods Program ↗

With Ashwini Deshpande and Shantanu Khanna

Studies the expansion of women’s self-help groups across India and their effects on rural female labour-force participation and the shift from casual work into self-employment.

Ongoing Development

Refugee and Host Integration through the Safety Net

An impact evaluation in Ethiopia studying livelihoods, social cohesion, and how contact changes the diffusion of prejudice through social networks.

With Dennis Egger, Alfredo Manfredini-Böhm, Belay Mohammed, and Sandra Rozo

Ongoing Labour

Firm Responses to Workplace Harassment Legislation

Models how sexual harassment distorts firms’ gender composition and productivity, and evaluates the unintended effects of India’s workplace legislation.

With Sonia Bhalotra, Kanika Mahajan, Fan Wang, and Medha Chatterjee

Ongoing Gender

Legislative Change and Female Employment

Studies state-level reforms to women’s night-shift restrictions in Indian factories and how firm size mediates their effect on women’s employment.

With Bhanu Gupta, Kanika Mahajan, and Anisha Sharma

MPhil thesis Environment

Economic Inclusion Programmes and Climate Shocks

Evaluates whether a nationwide poverty-graduation programme in Ethiopia helps households adapt to persistent drought. Awarded a distinction at Oxford.
